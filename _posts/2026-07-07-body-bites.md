### The Body Bites Back 

Forget runners' high.

I'm backflipping through physical exhaustion 
while devastating medical professionals 
explain away my personal experience.

how can this Body be crumbling ruins?

a lifelong journey of battling sport's complex balance

craving \
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; support\
a foothold

come back to me, Health.
